CHARLES'S AGENT // SLOT 01

Hush

Duelist

A telekinetic Duelist from Guangdong, China who redirects utility in midair to flash, displace, and suspend opponents.

Player
Charles
Role
Duelist
Origin
Guangdong, China

Hush's abilities

Basic · Q

Flashbulb

Throw or prepare the flash, then redirect it toward your crosshair. It flashes players looking at it when it bursts.

Signature · E

Displacement

Guide a thrown charge to pull nearby players inward. Players who collide with a surface or another player are concussed; the ability resets every two kills.

Basic · C

Flow

Create an anchor and propel toward it after a delay. If Hush is airborne, she briefly levitates first.

Ultimate · X

Suspension

Steer a projectile with your crosshair, then detonate it to lift players caught in its area.

Passive

Telekinesis

Pick up weapons, the Spike on attack, and interact with switches from an increased range.

Alternative usage of her skills

Hush can redirect her guided utility midair, letting her flash or displace enemies from unexpected angles.
