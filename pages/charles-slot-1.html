Waylay's abilities

Lightspeed

Equip a burst of speed and dash forward twice. Alt fire uses one dash; only the first dash can send Waylay upward.

Refract

Place a marker at Waylay's position. Activate again to return to it as a mote of light.

Saturate

Equip and throw a cluster of light that bursts on ground impact, hindering nearby enemies with a powerful movement and weapon slow.

Ultimate · X

Convergent Paths

Project a beam of light, then gain a speed boost as it expands and hinders enemies in its path.

Fast engage to disengage

Use Saturate to slow opponents before dashing in with Lightspeed. Place Refract before taking a fight so Waylay can return to safety afterward.
