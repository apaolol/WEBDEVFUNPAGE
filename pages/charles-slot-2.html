CHARLES'S AGENT // SLOT 02

Iso

Duelist

A Chinese fixer for hire who shapes ambient energy into a bulletproof shield and challenges opponents to focused duels.

Player
Charles
Role
Duelist
Origin
Chongqing, China

Iso's abilities

Basic · Q

Undercut

Send a molecular bolt through the battlefield to suppress and make vulnerable every player it touches.

Basic · C

Contingency

Push an indestructible wall of prismatic energy forward to block bullets. Alt fire sends it at a slower speed.

Signature · E

Double Tap

Channel Focus to gain a shield that absorbs one instance of damage. Shooting an energy orb refreshes Focus and the shield.

Ultimate · X

Kill Contract

Send a zone forward that pulls Iso and the first enemy hit into an isolated duel.

Passive

Focus

While Focused, Iso reloads faster. Enemies he damages or kills can leave destructible energy orbs; shooting an orb refreshes Focus and grants a shield.

Double Disadvantage

Iso can activate Double Tap before using Kill Contract to enter the isolated duel with a shield. Kill Contract also restores his health during the effect, making it useful when Iso is low.
