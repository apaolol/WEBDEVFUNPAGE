Gekko's abilities

Mosh Pit

Throw Mosh, who duplicates upon landing and spreads across a large area before exploding, damaging enemies caught inside.

Wingman

Send Wingman forward to concuss enemies. He can also plant or defuse the Spike when Gekko has it in his possession.

Dizzy

Launch Dizzy into the air. She fires plasma blasts at enemies in her line of sight, blinding them temporarily.

Thrash

Take control of Thrash and guide her toward enemies. Detonate to leap forward and detain opponents caught in the explosion.
