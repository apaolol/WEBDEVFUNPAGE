SYMOND'S AGENT

Gekko

Initiator

An Initiator from Los Angeles, USA, who leads a close-knit crew of creatures called buddies. His companions help gather information, disrupt enemies, and create opportunities for his team.

Player
Symond
Role
Initiator
Country

Gekko's abilities

Basic

Mosh Pit

Throw Mosh, who spreads across an area and explodes, damaging enemies caught inside.

Basic

Wingman

Send Wingman forward to concuss enemies. He can also plant or defuse the Spike when Gekko has it.

Signature

Dizzy

Launch Dizzy into the air. She fires plasma blasts at enemies in her line of sight, blinding them temporarily.

Ultimate

Thrash

Take control of Thrash and guide her toward enemies. Detonate to leap forward and detain opponents caught inside.

Using Gekko's Buddies

Use Dizzy to blind enemies before entering a site, Wingman to help plant or defuse the Spike, and Thrash to detain opponents. Recover your buddies whenever possible to use them again.
