SYMOND'S AGENT

Sage

Sentinel

A Chinese Sentinel who creates safety for her team through healing, defensive barriers, and slowing enemies. Sage is capable of protecting teammates and bringing fallen allies back into the fight.

Player
Symond
Role
Sentinel
Country

Sage's abilities

Basic

Slow Orb

Throw a slowing orb that creates a field of ice upon landing, slowing players who move through it.

Basic

Healing Orb

Heal an injured ally or restore your own health using the Healing Orb.

Signature

Barrier Orb

Create a solid wall that blocks enemy movement and helps protect important areas.

Ultimate

Resurrection

Revive a fallen teammate, bringing them back into the fight with full health.

Protecting the Team

Use Barrier Orb to block enemy pushes, Slow Orb to delay incoming attackers, and Healing Orb to support injured teammates. Save Resurrection for an important opportunity to bring a teammate back into the round.
